## Scope and precedence

The full archive contributes product requirements, engineering contracts, research leads and acceptance fixtures. Current human instructions settle conflicts: Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails are first-party vanilla scope; **modded read-only discovery and isolated pY performance benchmarking begin during Helper development**, but live third-party gameplay automation/compatibility qualification remains R20. Three modes share planning services: Helper observes and plans with **zero gameplay writes**; existing supervised safety regressions are retained; expansion of Semi-Autonomous work requires **Gate H1**; Full stays locked until its later independent gates. Always retain emergency stopping, scoped authorization and the five-tile development movement limit. Model prompts cannot change permissions. See [Helper-first and Gate H1](HELPER_FIRST_GATE_H1.md).

Use legitimate research, inventory, crafting, build reach, energy, force, surface and charted knowledge. Never expose arbitrary Lua/RCON, shell execution, spawning, teleportation or self-modifying permissions to the agent. Explicitly distinguish controlled test fixtures from earned gameplay evidence. Stop must cancel owned character actions and new dispatch; independent bots, trains, projectiles and world simulation need honest capability-specific handling.

Old archive status notes are snapshots. They do not undo published MIT/attribution, safety work, memory, navigation, bounded map/inventory, starter operations, mode enforcement or movement fixes. Compatibility tables and dated release claims are research leads, not certification. Historical kickoff prompts do not authorize code changes, installs, game actions, publishing or automations.

## Delivery and acceptance requirements

Each row requires an implementation plus reproducible evidence before completion. R01–R20 milestone IDs and G0–G9 existing gates remain; **Gate H1 is an additional user-reviewed product readiness gate**, not a renumbering. The binding delivery order is: preserve safety foundation; finish R04–R06 telemetry, AI Helper, dashboard; implement R07 and read-only R08 Blueprint Studio after pre-R07 data/math/safety readiness; add provenance-checked community/YouTube blueprint research and bounded mod discovery; pass H1; only then expand Semi-Autonomous execution (R08–R16); enable Full only after later independent gates and authorization. The [detailed H1 contract](HELPER_FIRST_GATE_H1.md) governs edge cases.

## Gate mapping and evidence record

G0 maps to safe control and progress verification; G1/G2 to legitimate beginner factory and sustained science; G3/G4 to physical blueprints, bots, circuits and trains; G5/G6 to platform and quality proof; G8 to vanilla/Space Age victories; G9 to endurance. G7 is SE and runs only in Phase 6 despite its number. The existing reusable test save remains for routine regression; fresh-start, version and destructive-failure fixtures use separately authorized isolated scenarios.

For each fixture record requirement ID, exact game/content/runtime versions, source/snapshot fingerprints, test type, initial state, authorization scope, expected transitions, observed deltas, failures/recovery, costs, timing and Stop outcome. Design/reference availability, unit tests, source smoke tests, frozen-app tests and live/endurance evidence are separate levels. A broad task is not completed by a single narrow fixture.
